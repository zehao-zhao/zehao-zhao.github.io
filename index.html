Research Highlights

My research focuses on adaptive intelligence: proactive AI agents and language models that continue to learn, anticipate, reason, and coordinate after deployment. The goal is to make adaptation persistent, safe, and reliable—new capabilities should be acquired at test time, retained across long contexts, and improved through interaction without sacrificing alignment, robustness, stability, or efficiency.

Across LLM post-training, multi-agent game theory, test-time adaptation, and systems optimization, I pursue one question: how can an AI system keep getting better after it enters the world?

  • Persistent Adaptation: Building test-time learning, recursive self-improvement, on-policy distillation with black-box teachers, and long-context plasticity mechanisms that acquire new behavior while measuring retention, locality, and generalization.
  • Proactive & Strategic Agents: Developing situation-aware agents while studying bounded-rational equilibria, cooperative self-evolution, and preference optimization for agents that learn from—and against—other decision-makers.
  • Efficient Systems for Evolving Models: Co-designing post-training and inference through multi-teacher distillation, efficient KV caches, and heterogeneous compute systems that make continuous adaptation practical.
